FAQ

The questions people ask first.

Q.Does the token mean I legally own the land?
No. A real company (an SPV) holds the actual legal title, the normal way, through the county. The token is a share in that company. Owning the token means owning a piece of what the company owns.
Q.So what does the blockchain actually do?
It makes buying and selling your share fast, easy, and visible to everyone — instead of slow paperwork, it's one transaction. The legal title itself never moves on-chain.
Q.What does the AI actually verify?
It reads the submitted documents — lease, invoice, title, deed — and checks that the details match and make sense (dates, amounts, parties). It then gives a risk score with its reasoning, instead of a black-box number.
Q.Is this legally binding right now?
This is a hackathon demo. The flow and structure are real and modeled on how live RWA projects work, but no SPV, license, or legal entity exists yet behind this build.
Q.Why X Layer?
Low fees and fast settlement, which matters when assets are traded in small shares rather than one expensive lump sum.
Q.Does this only work for one country?
No. The verification and SPV structure is designed to repeat in any market — the AI checks documents, not geography.
